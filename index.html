Coast Guard Memorandum Generator

MemoCraft is a free, unofficial browser tool for U.S. Coast Guard Academy cadets to format U.S. Coast Guard memorandums (memos) and export them to Microsoft Word or PDF. It is not affiliated with the U.S. Coast Guard or the Department of Homeland Security.
